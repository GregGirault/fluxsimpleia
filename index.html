How I test

Method

  1. Keep the exact prompt or workload fixed.
  2. Record requested model, route, settings and client version.
  3. Measure latency, failures, retries and successful completion.
  4. Separate provider limits from client and tool failures.
  5. Compare cost per completed job, not headline price alone.

What I do not assume

  • One successful request does not prove reliability.
  • Published documentation does not prove measured performance.
  • A large context window does not mean all of it is useful for your workload.
  • A low nominal price can lose its advantage after retries and failures.
