How to interpret the report

One CSV row describes a batch of jobs for a specific provider, model, client version and workload. Attempts includes retries, while successful_jobs and failed_jobs count final outcomes. Enter the total API bill, including failed attempts, in api_spend_usd.

The tool groups data by workload, model, client version and provider. Cost per successful job divides the total bill by completed successful jobs. Failure rate counts failed final outcomes. Retry attempts are the difference between attempts and final outcomes.

Latency warning: a pooled p95 cannot be calculated from pre-aggregated per-batch p95 numbers. We therefore show the range of reported batch p95 values, not a fabricated overall percentile. For fair provider comparisons, keep prompts, sampling conditions and model settings equivalent.

No supplier rankings or recommendations are inferred from a user-provided CSV alone. Validate data provenance and service terms before relying on financial conclusions.
